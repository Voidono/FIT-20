# Endings

The first resolved boss encounter selects the route permanently for this save.
Defeat gives the normal ending; Spare gives the hidden ending. Both rescue the
faculty head and permit the same Stone #4 handoff. Combat points remain exclusive
to victory. Old saves with an already completed boss default to the normal route.

After receiving Stone #4, interact with the 210C door again and choose to activate
the Time Gate. Its four slots save placement individually, require actual stone
ownership and allow returning to the office. Both routes share the gate screen.

The current epilogues are text-based. The hidden one has CON BUG repair the gate,
the return to 2006, an anniversary programme keepsake and the final joke. Completing
it saves `hidden_ending_complete` and `badge_debug_together`; the normal one saves
`normal_ending_complete`. Both save `ending_complete` and complete the gate quest.
The closing screen has an explicit return-to-menu button. New Game clears the
ending route and badge with the rest of progress.

This supersedes the defeat-only Spare policy in earlier reviews and CHAPTER4.md.
The gate UI is a functional text epilogue, not a finished 2006 map or production
credits sequence. User-managed floor transitions remain outside this change.
